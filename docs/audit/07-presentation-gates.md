# Research and slide preparation gates

Run `bun scripts/audit-presentation-gates.ts` from the workspace root. It locates count limits, time and size budgets, policy branches and degradation paths. The output is a review list. Compare each gate with what the host requested, what the worker recorded and what was saved.

The reported private project had nine verified research sections and 68 evidence records. Its historical preparation for ten slides saved one slide as completed. Earlier traces showed the planner proposing enough beats in some attempts, followed by grounding and template rejection. The worker published a verified subset and later requests could reuse it as a cached success.

| Gate | Behavior and disposition |
|---|---|
| Slide count in [planner](../../backend/src/services/presentation-planner.ts) and [job repository](../../backend/src/repositories/deck-preparation.ts) | The planner receives the exact requested count. The job checks the research ID, count and render-review result before commit. The versioned cache key keeps historical decks accessible and excludes them from new generation. |
| Readiness in [Decisions adapter](../../backend/src/services/presentation-readiness.ts) | The 0.65 probability cutoff routes to planning or one bounded gap pass. It is a routing parameter. Every material slide claim still needs independent verification. Calibration against a diverse labeled project set is open. |
| Evidence selection in [planner](../../backend/src/services/presentation-planner.ts) | A byte budget prioritizes verified citations and includes additional eligible source passages. Distinct findings can come from the same section or passage. Files-only bundles exclude public passages. |
| Rejected beats in [planner](../../backend/src/services/presentation-planner.ts) | Checked content can inform a targeted repair. A short or unavailable result ends with an actionable error or a follow-up research request. Historical duration-based scenarios retain their compatibility path. |
| Numerical templates in [planner](../../backend/src/services/presentation-planner.ts) | Self-contained metrics can stand without a redundant highlight. Wrapped PDF lines can support label and value binding. Unsupported or swapped values still fail. |
| Follow-up limits and source provenance in [research agent](../../backend/src/services/research-agent.ts) and [deck worker](../../backend/src/workers/deck-preparation.ts) | A single gap pass is checkpointed inside the deck job. A brief with links and reviewed public searches can use both during gap recovery. A link-only brief stays focused and files-only skips source lookups. Additional material is validated and used only in the new deck. A live Apollo 11 check recovered the landing date and moonwalk duration, then rendered all three slides. |
| Discovery and checklist handoff in [research agent](../../backend/src/services/research-agent.ts) and [research service](../../backend/src/services/research.ts) | Discovery leaves time for synthesis and fact-checking. A checklist draft can address a source-backed omission found in review. Comparable cited tables can be checked for a chart even when synthesis skipped its chart proposal. Unverified chart proposals leave a visible gap. |
| Partial fact and chart reviews in [grounding](../../backend/src/services/research-grounding.ts) and [graphs](../../backend/src/services/research-graphs.ts) | Incomplete fact-check replies receive a bounded retry. Each published chart needs complete decisions for its own units. The standalone research verifier still has a whole-review size ceiling. Batching very large research documents remains open. |
| Browser review in [render review](../../backend/src/services/deck-render-review.ts) | Render checks and layout repair use at most three passes. A lease lost after render begins ends with an explicit retry. |
| Extraction limits in [page reader](../../backend/src/services/host-pages.ts) and [source limits](../../backend/src/constants/sources.ts) | HTML, plaintext, PDFs and candidate links remain bounded. A source can hide relevant evidence past a cap. Reporting how much a read omitted and ranking across unusually large source windows remains open. |
| Reporting in [handoff checker](../../scripts/check-handoff.ts) and [agent audit](06-codex-agents.md) | Verified rows must match the required evidence class. Agents still open the saved brief, rendered slides and narration for an output-quality decision. |
