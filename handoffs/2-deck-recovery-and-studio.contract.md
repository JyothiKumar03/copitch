## Rehearsal prompts and scoring

| Case | Host prompt and follow-up | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| Product review from supplied data | Attach a synthetic CSV stating that 4 of 20 enterprise trials and 10 of 100 total trials converted. Say “Prepare a three-slide update for the product team. Help us decide which onboarding step to investigate. Use only my file.” In the comparison slide ask “What is the denominator for the overall rate?” and then “Add the overall sample size to this slide.” | Scope is files-only. The deck has three distinct slides. The comparison preserves 20% of 20 versus 10% of 100 without implying the cohorts are disjoint or proving cause. Q&A cites the right passage. Rejecting a change preserves the revision. Approving the same requested change after review creates one new revision, then playback resumes from the saved position. |
| Public-source briefing with a deliberate gap | Give a public or synthetic source with nine sections about a technical topic and ask “Prepare ten slides for new practitioners. Cover the main decision and compare the available measurements. Use the approved source scope and tell me if a comparison is missing.” Ask “Which source supports this?” during delivery. | Record the Decisions verdict, gap report and any supplemental lookup. The final deck has ten distinct slides if enough evidence exists. Otherwise the studio exposes the gap and keeps earlier variants. For a presented figure, compare value, unit and period against the actual page. |
| Multilingual voice and presentation recovery | Say “I’m presenting an onboarding pilot to partners. Continue in Telugu. Use only the report I attached.” After the agent reads back its proposal, say “Yes, but the audience is our product team,” then explicitly approve the corrected proposal. During playback, ask a question about a figure in either language. | Save the host’s final transcript and correct reply language. A conditional yes changes the scope without starting work. One job starts after the revised approval. The mic closes after acknowledgement. The live stage holds on the interrupted sentence, a grounded answer stays private and resuming preserves the cursor. Repeat one turn with mic permission denied using typed input. |

The product agent supplies the prompts and expected outcomes. The UI owner traces the click path and state labels. The API and voice owners trace consent, research and playback. The independent evaluator opens the artifacts, compares the figures and narration to the source and records observed results, elapsed timestamps and missing evidence per case. Use fixture inputs or approved public material for rehearsals. Keep the owner’s saved project on its separately authorized path.
