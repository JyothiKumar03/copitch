| AC-1 | Sign in and create a project | The private studio opens with chat and files in the center and the agent on the right on desktop. | AC-2 | Open another customer's pitch URL | The request is denied, and the other customer's project and pitch are absent from the signed-in project list. | Save the URL of account A’s pitch. Sign in as B, open Projects, and request A’s saved pitch URL using B’s credentials. | fixture |
| AC-3 | Brief the agent in two languages | The microphone stays active across turns, the transcript preserves both languages and a spoken interruption cuts off the reply. | In the studio enable voice once. Speak an English turn, interrupt the agent with a Hindi turn and ask a mixed English/Hindi follow-up. Check the spoken reply language and interruption on a physical microphone. | live |
| AC-4 | Choose files-only research and approve a brief | The studio displays the confirmed scope, runs one job and limits retrieval to the attached material. | Attach a CSV, select My materials only, submit a brief, approve the displayed scope twice and inspect the job count and recorded outbound searches. | fixture |
| AC-5 | Ask for progress during research | The agent answers from the in-progress job and the same job continues. | In the studio start approved research, ask for progress by voice before completion, and compare job identity and confirmed revision before and after the answer. | live |
| AC-6 | Select the completed pitch file | The private viewer opens the saved pitch, previews the cited chart and exposes Markdown source and download for that revision. | In the studio open a completed fixture pitch with comparable source data. Select pitch.md in chat, inspect chart values and evidence references, switch to Markdown, download the file and compare to the displayed source. Open the memo separately. | fixture |
| AC-7 | Close the browser during research and return | The signed-in host finds the same project, one research result and its saved pitch. Microphone capture requires a fresh gesture. | Start an approved job, close the tab, restart its worker, then sign in from a fresh browser. Reopen the project, wait for the result and open pitch.md. Check saved sources, revision and job count. | fixture |
| AC-8 | Deny microphone permission | The studio offers typed input and the host can finish the same brief, approve research and obtain the pitch. | In a fresh studio deny the microphone prompt, enter a brief, confirm the scope and open the completed file. | fixture |
| AC-9 | Open a pitch with unsupported chart data | Uncomparable values are withheld, and the remaining pitch and evidence gaps are readable. | In the studio approve a fixture with mixed units for the proposed comparison, open the generated pitch and compare its claims and chart area with the source rows. | fixture |
| AC-10 | Return to the hosted service | The account owner can complete the journey at its HTTPS address, sign out and sign back in from another browser to reopen the same pitch. | After owner-approved deployment, open the hosted home page, sign in, create a project, brief by voice, approve, download pitch.md, then sign in on a second device and reopen the same revision. | deployed |

Out of scope: Public live presentation, guided browser walkthrough, payment-plan selection and general chart messages outside the private pitch artifact.
Invariants: Account and project isolation, explicit source-scope approval, evidence provenance, revision identity, one durable job per approved brief, and recoverable host-only artifacts.
